# Most of Karpathy's 63 repositories are historical - do not read the tail as current advice

## What it is

Of 63 repositories, 9 are forks of other projects rather than his own work, 1 is archived (LLM101n, an unfinished course), and roughly 14 are legacy JavaScript, Lua or MATLAB machine-learning libraries last touched between 2013 and 2017 - convnetjs, recurrentjs, reinforcejs, tsnejs, svmjs, forestjs and similar.

## Why it matters

Star counts on those old libraries still look impressive, so a naive sweep would surface them as recommendations. They are of historical interest; the current work is the nano* and llm.c line.
