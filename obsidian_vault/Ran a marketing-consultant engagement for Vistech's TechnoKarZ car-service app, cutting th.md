# Ran a marketing-consultant engagement for Vistech's TechnoKarZ car-service app, cutting the quote 75% to win the deal

## What it is

Acting as marketing consultant to client 'Vistech,' scoped and priced a 2-phase GoMechanic-style car service app (Chennai, iOS/Android customer + mechanic apps, Firebase backend, membership tiers, in-app chat, emergency calls, inventory tracking). The quote was negotiated down from an initial ₹40.9 lakh full-feature estimate to a ₹26.5 lakh simplified version, then to a ₹6.6 lakh ultra-lean MVP (a 75% cut), before restoring some features at ₹8.5 lakh.

## Why it matters

This illustrates his recurring negotiation pattern of aggressively stripping scope and cost to make a deal winnable while keeping the client's must-have features — relevant to any future consulting/quoting engagement.
