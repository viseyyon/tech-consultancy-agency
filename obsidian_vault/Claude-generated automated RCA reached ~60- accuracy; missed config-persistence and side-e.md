# Claude-generated automated RCA reached ~60% accuracy; missed config-persistence and side-effect details

## What it is

A real production WiFi-outage RCA auto-generated by Claude (attributing the fault to a vendor HAL bug forcing radios off post-factory-reset via TCH_getExtOperMode failure) was validated by developers at ~60% accuracy: it correctly identified symptom and root vendor bug, but missed that /nvram/bbhm_bak_cfg.xml is the actual source of truth at WiFi-agent init, didn't establish one-time vs recurring failure, and proposed an SNMP-based workaround that would have broken split-SSID configurations.

## Why it matters

Automated RCA needs developer validation on config-layer persistence, temporal recurrence, and downstream side effects before any fix is deployed — a concrete accuracy ceiling to keep in mind for any RCA automation claim.
