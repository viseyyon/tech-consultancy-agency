# Top-management reports should shift from individual-contributor view to initiative/outcome view without cutting detail

## What it is

When Saravanan Moorthy requested a top-management-audience status report rebuild, the explicit instruction was to keep all detail but shift from an individual-contributor view to an initiative/outcome view. The rebuild removed the per-engineer section, expanded each initiative into this-sprint-vs-next-sprint blocks with embedded ticket ledgers, added a 12-metric KPI dashboard, and added priority-tagged risks with SLA-linked actions.

## Why it matters

Establishes the reporting template to reuse for any future executive-audience status report: organize by initiative/outcome, not by name, without dropping ticket-level detail.
