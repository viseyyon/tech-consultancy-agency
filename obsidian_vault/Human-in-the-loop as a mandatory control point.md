# Human-in-the-loop as a mandatory control point

## What it is

Several sources treat a human review or approval step as a first-class architectural pattern, not a fallback, especially before an agent takes actions with real-world side effects.

## Why it matters

Reduces the blast radius of autonomous mistakes once an agent can actually write data or take actions, not just read.
