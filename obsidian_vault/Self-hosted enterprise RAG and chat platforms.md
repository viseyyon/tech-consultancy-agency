# Self-hosted enterprise RAG and chat platforms

## What it is

Onyx, formerly Danswer, is a self-hosted AI chat platform with retrieval and enterprise-search built in that works with any LLM backend; ai-knowledge-assistant is a smaller example combining advanced RAG with Claude/Ollama routing, a circuit breaker, and ReAct agents for a resilient self-hosted Q&A stack.

## Why it matters

Choose these over a hosted ChatGPT Enterprise or Glean subscription when data has to stay self-hosted.
