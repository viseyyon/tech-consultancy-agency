# Scraped star counts in this corpus are not reliable quality signals

## What it is

Several low-substance or narrowly-scoped repos in this corpus carry implausibly high star counts relative to their visible content or scope, such as a single-sentence agent repo at 234k-plus stars and a tiny CLI tool at tens of thousands of stars, consistent with scraping or attribution noise rather than genuine popularity.

## Why it matters

Do not use the star figures in this corpus as a proxy for quality or adoption without independently re-checking the actual repository.
