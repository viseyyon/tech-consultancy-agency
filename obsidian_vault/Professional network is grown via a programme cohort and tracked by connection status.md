# Professional network is grown via a programme cohort and tracked by connection status

## What it is

He maintains a professional network built substantially from a business-school programme cohort, and tracks progress in LinkedIn terms — connection requests sent, accepted, and pending — rather than by relationship depth. Message replies and some connection-request activity are partly automated by an AI browser assistant acting under his own LinkedIn persona, which also flags who tagged him so he can follow up.

## Why it matters

Explains that his networking is systematized and semi-automated, so requests to help draft outreach or track connections should fit that existing workflow rather than starting from scratch.
