# Voice-agent frameworks and platforms

## What it is

Pipecat is a widely-used open-source framework, maintained by Daily, for building real-time voice and multimodal conversational agents; Dograh is a platform-level product for building AI voice-calling agents on top of that kind of stack.

## Why it matters

Pipecat is the framework layer to build on; Dograh represents the packaged-product layer when you do not want to assemble the pipeline yourself.
