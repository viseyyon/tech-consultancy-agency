# Always credits the corporate platform layer (Copilot/Azure/GitHub owners) without using 'CoE' externally

## What it is

In external-facing or elevated-audience communication about AI work, he explicitly credits corporate platform-layer owners (e.g. James Krieger and Chris Coppa, who own Copilot LLM models, Azure AI Foundry, Azure cloud, GitHub base infrastructure) as the foundation his team builds on top of, framed as peer language ('build on top of') rather than dependency language ('rely on'). He deliberately avoids the term 'CoE' in external-facing messages, keeping that framing internal.

## Why it matters

A consistent communication pattern to replicate in future external messaging: credit platform owners as peers, and keep 'CoE' terminology internal-only.
