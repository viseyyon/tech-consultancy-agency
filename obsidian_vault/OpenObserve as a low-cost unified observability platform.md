# OpenObserve as a low-cost unified observability platform

## What it is

openobserve ingests logs, metrics, traces, frontend telemetry, and LLM or agent observability into one self-hosted backend, built in Rust with Parquet and S3 storage and native OpenTelemetry and PromQL support; promotional posts claim storage costs up to roughly 140x lower than Elasticsearch.

## Why it matters

Positioned as a cheaper, simpler alternative to Datadog, Splunk, or Elastic for teams wanting full-stack observability including LLM traces without per-GB pricing, though the 140x figure is a vendor claim.
