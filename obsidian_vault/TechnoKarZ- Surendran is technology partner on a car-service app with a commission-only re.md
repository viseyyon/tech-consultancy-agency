# TechnoKarZ: Surendran is technology partner on a car-service app with a commission-only revenue model

## What it is

TechnoKarZ is an automotive service/repair app for which Surendran acts as technology partner, targeting a build cost of ₹4,50,000 (excl. GST). Pricing was redesigned to charge 0% commission on regular maintenance service and commission only premium revenue streams (emergency 25%, premium services 20%, membership 15%, parts/accessories 12%). Pitch materials also outline 30+ passive-income add-ons and a white-label licensing plan for other service center owners; specific ROI/revenue figures are marketing projections, not verified numbers.

## Why it matters

Structuring commission around premium streams only avoids burdening day-to-day service center operations, making the pitch easier to sell to owners.
