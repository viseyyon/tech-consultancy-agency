# Vantiva's AI CoE force-restructured into a unified 'AIOps' team after losing ~44% of DevOps headcount

## What it is

Sir's four sub-teams (~16 people) lost 7 members (attrition + rebadge) heading into April 2026, including both DevOps sub-team leads. The final plan reframed the loss as a deliberate transformation: DevOps and AI functions merged into one 'AIOps' org under six functional clusters (Android, RDK/Base/Linux, Homeware, Signings, RDK BB/Telco, Evolvere).

## Why it matters

The single highest residual risk flagged is that RDK Broadband/Telco coverage now rests on one newly-trained engineer with zero platform experience and no dedicated mentor, since the two experienced engineers (Arthi Rathna, Praveen Natarajan) were moved to Android instead.
