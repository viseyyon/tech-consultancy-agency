# Data finding: irrelevant JIRA clarifying questions slow ticket closure up to 283%, relevant ones speed it 76%

## What it is

An AI-powered analysis of the SWIDOPS team's Jira comments found only 1.4-15.4% of 'question' comments were actually relevant to ticket content (avg relevance 31.4/100). Relevant questions closed tickets 76% faster; irrelevant ones (the common case) slowed closure by up to 283%, net-slowing the team's overall closure by 22.3%. Six of seven engineers were flagged for training on ticket-specific questioning.

## Why it matters

Gives a quantified basis for targeting question-asking training rather than treating all clarifying questions as equally useful.
