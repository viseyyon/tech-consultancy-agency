# Vendor and community skill-pack ecosystems for coding agents

## What it is

Google and Vercel publish official first-party skill packs for their own products via google/skills and vercel-labs/agent-skills; community projects like Orchestra-Research's AI-Research-SKILLs, muratcankoylan's context-engineering skills, and affaan-m's everything-claude-code/ECC bundle broader skill, memory, and security layers on top of a general coding agent.

## Why it matters

Check official vendor skill packs first for product-specific correctness, and reach for community bundles when a broader capability upgrade such as context engineering, memory, or guardrails is needed beyond a single vendor's scope.
