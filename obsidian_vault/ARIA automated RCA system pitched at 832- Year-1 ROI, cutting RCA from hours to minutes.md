# ARIA automated RCA system pitched at 832% Year-1 ROI, cutting RCA from hours to minutes

## What it is

ARIA (Automated Root-cause Investigation & Analysis) is Viseyyon/Vantiva's AI-powered RCA system for RDK-B/embedded logs, built on ML techniques (TF-IDF, DBSCAN, isolation forest) combined with multi-agent LLM orchestration, reducing manual RCA time from 4-8 hours to 15-30 minutes and removing SME dependency. It was packaged with an executive ROI case of 832% Year-1 ROI, 39-day payback, and $1.35M 3-year NPV -- a business-case projection for the pitch, not a measured post-deployment result.
