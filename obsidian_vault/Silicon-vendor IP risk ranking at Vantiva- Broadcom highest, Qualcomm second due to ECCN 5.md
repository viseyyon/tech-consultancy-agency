# Silicon-vendor IP risk ranking at Vantiva: Broadcom highest, Qualcomm second due to ECCN 5E002

## What it is

Across a six-vendor chipset dependency analysis (Broadcom 97 families, Qualcomm ~22, Airoha/MediaTek ~14, MaxLinear ~16, Cortina/Marvell 1, AirTies/MediaTek ~2), Qualcomm was flagged as the highest risk after Broadcom because its 5G FWA modem/baseband source is likely export-controlled under ECCN 5E002, requiring an export counsel opinion before any AI tooling touches SDX baseband source.

## Why it matters

Qualcomm baseband source carries a distinct export-control risk category beyond the general vendor-SLA/AI-tooling risk applying to all six vendors, so any AI tooling proposal touching Qualcomm SDX code needs export counsel sign-off first.
