# Validates designs through a multi-AI council, then wants one synthesis

## What it is

Before building anything non-trivial, the user runs the design past a council of other models (Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi, DeepSeek, Grok) via the browser-bridge tool, then wants the findings synthesized into one revised deliverable, never several raw model answers pasted back. The assistant is expected to weigh in and overrule the council where warranted, and say so explicitly rather than deferring silently.

## Why it matters

Handing back raw, unsynthesized council output, or silently deferring to it without independent judgment, defeats the point of getting a second opinion.
