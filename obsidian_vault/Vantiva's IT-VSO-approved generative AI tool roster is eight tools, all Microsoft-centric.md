# Vantiva's IT/VSO-approved generative AI tool roster is eight tools, all Microsoft-centric

## What it is

Under AUP section 2.9, the only IT-approved generative AI tools at Vantiva are Copilot Standard, GitHub Copilot ($19/user/month, N+1 approval), Copilot for O365 ($30/user/month, N+2 approval), Teams Premium ($7/user/month, N+1 approval), Sofia GPT/OnePlan, Responsive/RFPIO, Databricks AI/BI Genie and Agent Bricks, and Docusign AI Assisted Review. Public ChatGPT, public Claude, Gemini direct, and Cursor IDE are explicitly not approved.

## Why it matters

This is the compliance baseline recurring across Vantiva compliance decks -- any AI-tooling proposal or vendor-risk discussion at Vantiva must be checked against this exact approved list.
