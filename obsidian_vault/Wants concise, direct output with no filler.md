# Wants concise, direct output with no filler

## What it is

The user prefers terse, direct answers over padded prose; saying nothing is preferred to filler, and words that don't change the point should be cut. This applies to normal conversational replies, not just deliverables.

## Why it matters

Verbose hedging or padding wastes the user's attention and works against an explicitly stated preference.
