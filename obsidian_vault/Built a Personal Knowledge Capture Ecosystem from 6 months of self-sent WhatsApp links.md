# Built a Personal Knowledge Capture Ecosystem from 6 months of self-sent WhatsApp links

## What it is

Built from 6 months of self-sent WhatsApp links: an AI-analysis prompt classifying content into 10 types and tagging by topic/priority, a Python parsing script, Notion (8 pre-configured views) and Obsidian (Dataview dashboards) templates, a Chrome extension (Ctrl+Shift+K quick capture) as a replacement for the WhatsApp-to-self habit, and a weekly digest generator producing prioritized reading lists that flag items stale beyond 30 days.

## Why it matters

A complete, already-built personal knowledge system he can keep using instead of the ad hoc WhatsApp-to-self habit, with a stale-item flag to prevent link rot.
