# Stakeholder BK requires weekly status reports to cover every AI stream and back claims with data

## What it is

Feedback from stakeholder BK on an earlier weekly report version was explicit: include all AI streams (not a subset) and support efficiency claims with actual data rather than assertions. This became a standing constraint applied across subsequent weekly-report iterations -- e.g. surfacing a previously unreported third project line (CG/Comcast Gateway) once discovered in source Excel data, and itemizing patches/requests with JIRA IDs rather than summarizing.

## Why it matters

Any future weekly report to BK must cover every AI stream and cite data/JIRA IDs rather than summarized claims, or it will draw the same feedback again.
