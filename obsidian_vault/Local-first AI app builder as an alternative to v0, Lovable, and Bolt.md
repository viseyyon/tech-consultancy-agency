# Local-first AI app builder as an alternative to v0, Lovable, and Bolt

## What it is

dyad-sh/dyad generates full applications from prompts and runs entirely on the user's own machine rather than as a hosted cloud service.

## Why it matters

Choose this over v0, Lovable, Replit, or Bolt when AI app generation is needed without a cloud subscription or sending code off-machine.
