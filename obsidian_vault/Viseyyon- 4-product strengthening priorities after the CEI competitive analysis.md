# Viseyyon: 4-product strengthening priorities after the CEI competitive analysis

## What it is

Following the CEI battlecard analysis, Viseyyon's roadmap concentrates on four products and deprioritizes the long tail of a 37-product catalog: (1) KNOWLEDGE-FORGE -- needs a defensible RCA benchmark and self-serve repo ingestion to move from consulting to product motion; (2) SYNTH-GUARD + AIRLOCK merged into one 'Compliance Plane' product with an ISO 42001/EU AI Act mapping document; (3) ANVIL/SYNTH-MEND -- strengthen the evidence pack with measured MTTR/defect-resolution rates to counter CEI's unverifiable SLA claims; (4) FORGE Hub -- mature on-prem/air-gapped packaging. A new packaged offer -- a fixed-fee 2-week Engineering Graph PoV with an evidence pack -- was proposed alongside.

## Why it matters

This is the current, ranked product-investment decision driving which of Viseyyon's products get near-term attention.
