# Sir's executive deck standard: 3-slide max, white background, infographic-heavy, real live data over estimates

## What it is

Across repeated deck-building sessions, his stated preference is maximum 3-slide decks for executive presentations so he can do most of the talking with slides only as guides, white backgrounds over dark themes for most deliverables, infographic-heavy visuals over text-dense tables, and pulling real live data (e.g. from the Evolvere platform) rather than estimated figures wherever possible.

## Why it matters

This is the standing template to apply to any future executive deck: 3 slides max, white background, infographic-heavy, live data over estimates.
