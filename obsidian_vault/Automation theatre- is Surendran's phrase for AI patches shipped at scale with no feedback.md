# "Automation theatre" is Surendran's phrase for AI patches shipped at scale with no feedback loop

## What it is

Coined in a leadership reply about the low patch-feedback rate: "generating patches into a void — that's not AI-assisted development, that's automation theatre." Used to argue that visible AI output without a closed feedback loop is not real progress, mirroring his other coined-phrase style (e.g. "sell outcomes not features").

## Why it matters
