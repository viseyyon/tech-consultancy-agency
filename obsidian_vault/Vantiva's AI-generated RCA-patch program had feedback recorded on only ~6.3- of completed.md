# Vantiva's AI-generated RCA/patch program had feedback recorded on only ~6.3% of completed patches (8 of 126)

## What it is

As of mid-March 2026, 126 RCA & Patch tickets were completed but only 8 had developer feedback recorded, and only 1 of those was positive-with-improvements. Leadership flagged this as unacceptable and imposed a mandatory feedback-closure-gate policy (no ticket closes without feedback) with a 2-week retroactive backfill of all 126.

## Why it matters

Without closed feedback, the AI patch program cannot improve — this triggered a policy change to force feedback capture.
