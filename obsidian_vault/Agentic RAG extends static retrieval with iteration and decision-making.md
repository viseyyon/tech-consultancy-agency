# Agentic RAG extends static retrieval with iteration and decision-making

## What it is

Beyond single-shot retrieve-then-generate, 'agentic RAG' lets the system decide whether and how many times to retrieve, route queries, and chain retrieval steps; the pattern is reaching niche surfaces too - on-device mobile RAG and a domain-specific multi-agent physics-simulation variant (MCP-SIM).

## Why it matters

Explains why recent RAG quality gains come from added control flow, not just better embeddings.
