# Political communication playbook: reframe rather than refuse, credit generously while claiming architecture quietly

## What it is

For inbound asks that risk narrative capture or premature disclosure, the working framework is: reframe rather than refuse outright, make slowing things down sound like helping the other party, give credit generously to collaborators while quietly retaining architecture ownership, and use governance/process language rather than permission-seeking language — staying in 'receive-mode' to understand the actual ask before sharing details, and routing sensitive asks through direct manager Ashwani Saigal when the audience is elevated.

## Why it matters

This is his standing playbook for handling marketing, cross-BU, or executive/investor requests that could leak roadmap or architecture details, so future drafting of such responses should follow this pattern by default.
