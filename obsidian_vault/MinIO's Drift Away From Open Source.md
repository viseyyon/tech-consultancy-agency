# MinIO's Drift Away From Open Source

## What it is

MinIO - commonly used as the S3-compatible object store behind self-hosted RAG/vector-store stacks - has been moving away from a permissively open-source distribution model, with reporting describing its repo status changing repeatedly as it pushes toward its commercial offering.

## Why it matters

Teams that picked MinIO assuming it was a stable free dependency should re-check current licensing and availability before relying on it long-term.
