# A usable ChatGPT-class system is claimed at around $100 of compute

## What it is

nanochat is described by its author as the best ChatGPT that one hundred dollars can buy, and llama2.c performs Llama 2 inference in a single file of pure C. Both are deliberate demonstrations that the compute floor for a working system is far lower than assumed.

## Why it matters

Useful as a cost anchor when someone claims serious training requires a cluster - but the claim is about a small model that demonstrates the pipeline, not a frontier-quality one.
