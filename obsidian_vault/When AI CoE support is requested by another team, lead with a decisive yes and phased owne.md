# When AI CoE support is requested by another team, lead with a decisive yes and phased ownership, not an open offer

## What it is

When another team requests AI CoE support (e.g. Automatics test-failure classification), the preferred framing is to state a confident 'yes, this aligns with our roadmap' up front, then structure the engagement into clear phases (architecture/feasibility, then build) with explicit ownership boundaries and timelines, positioning AI CoE as a strategic leader driving the initiative rather than a reactive service desk.

## Why it matters

This is the reusable response pattern for any future cross-team support request: lead decisively, then phase the work, rather than offering an open-ended commitment.
