# Calibrates message tone to the recipient's seniority

## What it is

For emails/messages to senior stakeholders (e.g. a CIO), the user wants a brief acknowledgment sent immediately, with substantive content following as a separate, later message. Tone should be more deferential upward, and the assistant should never grade a senior stakeholder's approach or direct peers on the user's behalf.

## Why it matters

Sending one combined reply, or an assertive/peer-toned message to someone senior, misreads the organizational register the user is managing.
