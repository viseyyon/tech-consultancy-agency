# ANVIL ROI must amortize setup cost and human review time — a raw token-cost comparison once inflated ROI to 487.9x

## What it is

An earlier ANVIL AI_OPS ROI figure of 487.9x was found to be wrong because it compared only LLM token costs against a flat manual-hours assumption, ignoring the one-time $5,000 pilot setup cost and human review time. The corrected model amortizes all costs and tells the honest story as 'breakeven at flow 14, system X% mature, cost-per-flow falling' — by 2026-07-08 the live dashboard showed $25.06 recurring cost/ticket vs a $400 manual baseline (93.7% reduction), $2,000 setup paid back at ticket six, and 59% system maturity.

## Why it matters

Any future ANVIL or similar ROI presentation must use the amortized model, not the raw token-cost comparison, or it risks repeating the same inflated-ROI error.
