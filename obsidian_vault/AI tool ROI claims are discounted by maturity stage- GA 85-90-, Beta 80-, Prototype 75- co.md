# AI tool ROI claims are discounted by maturity stage: GA 85-90%, Beta 80%, Prototype 75% confidence factors

## What it is

Each AI tool's claimed hours-saved/ROI impact is multiplied by a maturity-based accuracy discount: GA (general availability) tools count at 85-90% of claimed savings, Beta at 80%, Prototype at 75%. Paired with validation methods (spot-check audits, user surveys, telemetry tracking) documented in a 20+ page discussion guide backing the executive ROI/IRR deck.

## Why it matters

Keeps hours-saved and ROI figures credible under executive/finance scrutiny by discounting unproven-stage tools.
