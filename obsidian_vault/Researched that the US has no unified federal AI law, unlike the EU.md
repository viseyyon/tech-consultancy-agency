# Researched that the US has no unified federal AI law, unlike the EU

## What it is

He looked into AI regulation and confirmed that, unlike the EU's unified AI Act, the United States has no single comprehensive federal AI law. Instead, US AI governance currently rests on the National AI Initiative Act of 2020 combined with a patchwork of state-level laws and executive orders.
