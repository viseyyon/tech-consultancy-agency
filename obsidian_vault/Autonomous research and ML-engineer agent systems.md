# Autonomous research and ML-engineer agent systems

## What it is

Several projects (karpathy/autoresearch, K-Dense-AI's agentic ML engineer, AutoResearchClaw, Hugging Face's ml-intern) point an agent loop at the full research cycle: reading papers, running training experiments, and in some cases drafting a paper, rather than just writing code to a prompt.

## Why it matters

Relevant to anyone exploring how far agentic automation can go in the experiment-run-analyze loop, though these are experimental rather than production-grade.
