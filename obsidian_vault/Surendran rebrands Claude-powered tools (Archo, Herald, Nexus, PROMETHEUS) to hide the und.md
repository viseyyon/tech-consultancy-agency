# Surendran rebrands Claude-powered tools (Archo, Herald, Nexus, PROMETHEUS) to hide the underlying AI vendor

## What it is

'Aider' was replaced with 'Archo' throughout an RDK-B Codegen management deck and 'Claude CLI' text was demoted/removed from routing tables to make the deck 'more convincing' and 'less Claude.' A Claude Code remote-bridge tool went through name iterations (Nexus -> Herald) specifically so end users of the bridged product would not know Claude powers it. The pattern recurs across HERMES, PROMETHEUS, and PROMETHEUS OMEGA — internal AI systems consistently get project-specific brand names rather than being labeled by their underlying model.

## Why it matters

This is a consistent, deliberate practice across multiple projects, so any future deliverable meant for management or white-label clients should be expected to need the same vendor-hiding treatment.
