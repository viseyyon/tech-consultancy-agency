# Automatic safety-alignment removal from open-weight models

## What it is

p-e-w/heretic automatically finds and ablates the internal directions responsible for refusal behavior in an open-weight model, removing censorship without manual intervention.

## Why it matters

Relevant to people who want an uncensored local model variant, but it is a dual-use tool that also strips legitimate safety behavior, so treat any downstream use with that in mind.
