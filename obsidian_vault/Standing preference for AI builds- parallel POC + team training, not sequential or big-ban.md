# Standing preference for AI builds: parallel POC + team training, not sequential or big-bang delivery

## What it is

Across several planning sessions for an internal AI code-review system, the consistently chosen delivery model is a proof-of-concept phase run in parallel with team upskilling, using existing hardware, before committing to a full multi-month development plan — chosen over sequential, agile-sprint-only, and big-bang delivery.

## Why it matters

Preferred because it validates risk earlier, avoids idle team time, and defers hardware capex until the approach is proven.
