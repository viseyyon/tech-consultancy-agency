# CEI (cei.ai) competitive battlecard vs Viseyyon

## What it is

A full-portfolio analysis of CEI's 8 products (Clairvoyance, DARTS, Prism, AIM-FIRE, MigrateIQ, eTWIN, Cosine Match, an ISO 42001-certified AI Governance Framework) found their key strengths are the ISO 42001 badge and a closed validate-build-operate services story, and their key weaknesses are a publicly architecture-light stack, services-DNA scaling with headcount, and zero engineering-systems depth (no firmware/embedded/telco/Gerrit/air-gapped coverage). eTWIN was flagged as the highest strategic overlap with Viseyyon's Company Brain concept, and a 10-section battlecard with objection handling and discovery landmines was delivered.
