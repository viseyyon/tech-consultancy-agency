# multica-ai is a two-product org where the reputational asset outweighs the code

## What it is

The organisation holds only six repositories. Two dominate: the multica orchestrator at ~47k stars and the andrej-karpathy-skills CLAUDE.md at ~205k. The rest - a CLI, a runtime shim, a Homebrew tap and a .github placeholder - are supporting scaffolding of tens of stars each.

## Why it matters

A working example of distribution strategy: a curated guidance file borrowing a respected name pulled four times the attention of the actual engineering product it sits beside.
