# Context engineering as a discipline distinct from prompt engineering

## What it is

Framed as actively curating what enters an agent's context window over a task's lifetime, deciding what to include, summarize, or drop, rather than just writing a good initial prompt.

## Why it matters

As tasks get longer and multi-step, what you keep in context matters more than how any single instruction is phrased.
