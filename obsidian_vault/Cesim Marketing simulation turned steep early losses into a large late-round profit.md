# Cesim Marketing simulation turned steep early losses into a large late-round profit

## What it is

In the Cesim Marketing business simulation (team initially called Ochre, later Helios Tech), the team posted large losses in Rounds 1-2 (roughly -€37M and -€34M operating loss) caused mainly by a 498.8% sales-forecast error and high fixed costs. After correcting forecasts and rebalancing admin/advertising spend, the team turned around to a €133.8M profit in Round 3 and a €402.8M operating profit in Round 4.
