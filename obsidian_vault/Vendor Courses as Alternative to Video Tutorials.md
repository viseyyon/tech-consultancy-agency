# Vendor Courses as Alternative to Video Tutorials

## What it is

For learners who want a syllabus rather than a video playlist: Microsoft's free 'Generative AI for Beginners' (18-lesson repo) and the University of Helsinki-backed 'Elements of AI' both give a structured, non-video path into generative AI fundamentals; pairing either with a free vendor certification (Google/AWS/Microsoft GenAI courses with certificates) adds a credential.

## Why it matters

Useful for onboarding non-specialists who prefer reading/exercises to hours of video, or who need a certificate for HR/compliance purposes.
