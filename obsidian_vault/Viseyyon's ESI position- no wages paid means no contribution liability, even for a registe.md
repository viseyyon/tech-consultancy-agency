# Viseyyon's ESI position: no wages paid means no contribution liability, even for a registered owner-director

## What it is

Viseyyon Technologies (OPC) Pvt Ltd (ESI Reg. 51002491520000999, Chennai) holds that under Section 39 of the ESI Act 1948, liability arises only on wages actually paid or payable, not registration status — holding even when the sole registered Insured Person is the owner-director who is also the person in charge, provided no remuneration was drawn. Supporting evidence: NIL salary register, bank statement showing no payroll debit, optional board resolution on deferred director remuneration.

## Why it matters

The standing recommendation is to file a NIL contribution return proactively each month while pre-revenue, to avoid repeated ESIC notices.
