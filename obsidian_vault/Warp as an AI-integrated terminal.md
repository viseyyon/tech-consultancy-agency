# Warp as an AI-integrated terminal

## What it is

Warp is an established terminal application that builds AI assistance and agentic workflow features directly into the terminal itself, rather than treating AI as a separate wrapper around a classic emulator.

## Why it matters

An alternative to iTerm2 or standard terminals for developers who want AI help without switching context out of the terminal.
