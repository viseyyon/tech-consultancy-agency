# Reports AI-engineering impact to leadership in man-hours, deliberately not dollar values

## What it is

Executive reporting for the AI Engineering Intelligence Platform and its 8 tools (targeting 13,200 hours/year, ~6.4 FTE recovered) is deliberately framed as capacity recovery ('we're not asking for budget, we're recovering capacity you've already paid for'), using hours rather than dollars as the primary metric because it is geography-neutral, lets finance apply their own hourly rates, and keeps him in control of the narrative rather than ceding it to a contestable currency conversion.

## Why it matters

This is a deliberate, reusable framing rule for any future executive AI-impact reporting: report hours recovered, not dollar values, to avoid finance pushback and stay narrative-neutral across geographies.
