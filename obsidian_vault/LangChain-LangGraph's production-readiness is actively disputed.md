# LangChain/LangGraph's production-readiness is actively disputed

## What it is

Tutorials keep teaching LangChain and LangGraph as the default way to build agents, while at least one practitioner video argues against using LangChain in production, citing abstraction overhead and debugging difficulty.

## Why it matters

Weigh LangChain's abstractions against a lighter custom orchestrator before defaulting to it for production systems.
