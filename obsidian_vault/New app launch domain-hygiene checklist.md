# New app launch domain-hygiene checklist

## What it is

Nico Burkart recommends putting a newly acquired app on its own domain separate from the main company site (so outside collaborators can work on it independently), adding two dedicated email-sending subdomains via a service like Resend so the primary domain never gets flagged as spam, and generating and submitting a sitemap.xml for search indexing.

## Why it matters

A concrete, reusable pre-launch checklist for domain separation, email deliverability, and SEO setup when standing up a new product.
