# Catalog of 20+ Agentic Design Patterns

## What it is

Antonio Gulli's 'Agentic Design Patterns' book and a companion 'Master ALL 20 Agentic AI Design Patterns' course both catalog recurring agent-construction patterns (planner-executor, reflection, tool-use, multi-agent handoff) as named, reusable building blocks rather than one-off architectures.

## Why it matters

Gives a shared vocabulary for describing an agent design instead of re-deriving structure from scratch each time.
