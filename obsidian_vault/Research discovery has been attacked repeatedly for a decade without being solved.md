# Research discovery has been attacked repeatedly for a decade without being solved

## What it is

Six separate repositories across roughly ten years target the same problem of finding and filtering relevant papers: arxiv-sanity-preserver, arxiv-sanity-lite, researchpooler, researchlei, covid-sanity and nipspreview. Most have not been pushed for years.

## Why it matters

Relevant because the AI Agent Project Blueprint asks you to build precisely this - an evidence-gathering research assistant. A decade of abandoned attempts is a warning about scope, and a source of prior art.
