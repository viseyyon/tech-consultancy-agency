# 'Hours saved' formula: Manual Equivalent Time minus AI-Assisted Actual Time, with a documented baseline

## What it is

The core formula in Vantiva AI Engineering weekly status reports is Hours Saved = Manual Equivalent Time − AI-Assisted Actual Time, aggregated per capability (e.g. RCA reports averaging ~3-3.4 hours saved each). The methodology is formalized with explicit term definitions, three baseline-establishment methods (historical benchmarking, sampling validation, team consensus — ranked by confidence), guardrails like a 50% rework-exclusion rule, and a quarterly recalibration schedule.

## Why it matters

Because leadership scrutinizes these numbers, this documented methodology pre-answers the 'how do you know it's N hours' question for any future status report.
